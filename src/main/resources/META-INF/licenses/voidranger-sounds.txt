Voidranger sound sources and processing
=======================================

All seven source files were provided by the user in
`崩铁/物品/生物与实体/怪物音效来源/`. The user confirmed that every listed
Freesound item is CC0. The original source files are retained unchanged in that
archive. Runtime assets are derived copies under
`assets/starrail_sim/sounds/voidranger/`.

Processing: FFmpeg 4.3.4 (existing Bilibili installation; no dependency was
installed), mono 44.1 kHz PCM intermediate, Vorbis quality 5 (about 96 kb/s),
sample peak normalized to approximately -6 dBFS. A 10 ms fade-in and 25 ms
fade-out were applied. Leading/trailing silence was trimmed only where the
FFmpeg silence scan identified a sustained quiet interval at -55 dBFS. The
short roar and dragon hurt sources were retained at their original durations.

The `sounds.json` entries use volume and pitch multipliers to distinguish the
four creatures and to keep ambient cues below attack cues. All 24 entries now
reference these CC0-derived assets; none reference the old combat cues or
vanilla `SoundEvents`.

Verification notes
------------------

FFmpeg decoded each original and each resulting OGG. Duration, channel layout,
sample rate, peak level, and sustained silence were checked with FFmpeg's input
metadata, `silencedetect`, and `volumedetect`. The local inspection did not
include subjective listening. Sound character above is selected from the
provided source titles and descriptions; this record does not claim that
background noise or voice artifacts were ruled out by audition.
